Privacy

Kept on your phone.

Scraps is a private scrapbook. Snaps, captions, pet names, and streaks are stored on the device. There is no public feed and no follower list.

Camera and photos

The camera is used so you can snap a polaroid inside the journal. The photo library is used when you add a picture you already have, and only if you choose to save a snap back to your library.

What stays local

  • Pet profiles, including name, species, and photo
  • Daily snaps, captions, frames, stickers, and location pins you add
  • Streaks, quests, badges, and appearance settings

What we don’t do

Scraps does not sell your journal, and it does not turn your pet’s photos into a social network. You can reset the journal from Settings on the device.
